# Company shutdown criteria

## Required outcome

The primary research target is a company that has actually ceased operating.
Layoffs, falling revenue, a strategic pivot, a stopped product, or an announced
future closure do not establish whole-company cessation. An insolvency filing
alone does not establish cessation either. Do not populate a shutdown list with
impact events merely to increase its size.

## Evidence fields

Record company identity, operational scope, cessation date and precision,
announcement date, source URL, supporting passage, and retrieval date. Separate
operational cessation from legal dissolution: the latter requires its own
evidence and may remain unknown. Preserve any later acquisition, restart, or
continuing service as a separate development.

A company/founder closure statement or substantive reporting that explicitly
confirms completed whole-company cessation can support the outcome. Search
snippets, inaccessible websites, inactive social profiles, and failed product
logins alone cannot. Read the underlying evidence before qualifying a company.

## Separate AI attribution

Evaluate closure and AI causation independently. An AI startup failing does not
prove AI competition caused its failure. Record whether AI competition or
replacement is directly attributed by the company, supported by independent
analysis, merely discussed in reporting, or unestablished. Preserve financing,
commercialization, management, and other alternative explanations.

The exact breadth of the AI-related collection remains an open product decision;
whole-company cessation is required regardless. Keep broader AI-business
failures separate from companies demonstrably displaced by AI.

## Existing data

The legacy `outcome: shutdown` field does not encode entity scope or independently
verified closure. It is insufficient as an automatic inclusion rule. The current
seed contains 29 companies and 30 events; only The Planet D has that outcome,
and its supplied evidence describes cessation of publication rather than
establishing whole-company closure. Retain historical impact records separately
while requalifying the shutdown collection. Do not silently relabel them or
claim this document changes the deployed interface or database.
