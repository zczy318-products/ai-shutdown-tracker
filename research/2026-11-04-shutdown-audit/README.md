# Whole-company shutdown audit

Checked on 2026-11-04. This is a research correction, not a database import or
production-status audit. The governing distinction is documented in
[shutdown criteria](../../docs/SHUTDOWN_CRITERIA.md).

## Existing seed

The 29-company, 30-event seed contains 19 mass-layoff events, seven announced
downsizing events, two pivots, one reversal, and one shutdown label. The sole
shutdown label is The Planet D, whose supplied evidence establishes cessation
of publication, not the legal company's entire operational status. Consequently,
none is qualified for a strict whole-company shutdown collection by this seed
audit alone. This is a statement about available evidence, not a claim that all
29 companies are currently operating.
