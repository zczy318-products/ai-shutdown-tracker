# Small-company and independent-team discovery — 2026-11-04

Six historical research examples broaden the large-company-heavy discovery set. They are not six proven AI-caused failures, not a representative sample, and not approved publication records. This research batch changes no database, application code, or prior research batch and does not publish records. Jev was not used.

| Candidate | Size evidence at relevant time | Event boundary | AI interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tailwind Labs | Four paid engineers; three cut. Total company size unknown. | Engineering reduction; framework not declared closed. | Direct founder claim that AI hurt documentation-driven commercial sales. |
| Tome | About 59 employees in April 2024; approximately 20% cut. | Company customer/product pivot with layoffs. | AI startup seeking paying B2B customers; AI displacement not established. |
| Sunshine | Approximately 15 employees in September 2025. | Reported wind-down and asset transfer to Dazzle; staff expected to move. | AI-business context; not 15 verified job losses. |
| Artifact | Eight-person product team reported; legal-company headcount unknown. | January 2024 wind-down announcement followed by Yahoo acquisition closed March 29. Standalone app discontinued; technology continued. | AI-product lifecycle, not evidence AI caused a company death. |
| Ghost Autonomy | CEO confirmed 100 workers affected in April 2024. | Company operational closure. | AI/autonomy startup financing and commercialization failure; no AI-displacement claim. |
| Yupp | Company profile lists 11–50; event-time exact headcount unknown. | March 2026 wind-down and scheduled April product access termination. | AI-business context; profile range is not an audited census. |

Only Tailwind is an AI-impact candidate supported by a directly retrieved founder causal claim. The other five remain `AI_business_context_only`. Company-level count evidence exists for three; two have team-only size evidence; one has only a profile range. Ghost and Tome are medium-size by a 51–250 employee threshold. Do not call every startup a small company or replace unknown headcount with an assumed size.

`candidates.json` preserves exact source URLs, publication/reporting versus event dates, short quotations, access state, headcount scope, alternative explanations and unresolved fields. None contains a `RecordInput`, human-verification claim or publication approval. Tailwind's exact layoff day is left unresolved because the founder's relative-day comment and trade reporting are not identical; the month is January 2026.

The original-source paths include a founder GitHub comment, a buyer's official acquisition announcement, and an official company LinkedIn wind-down notice. Other cases use direct founder interviews or reported emails. Important access limits are retained: the Tailwind trade article returned 403 after a usable indexed excerpt; Ghost's former domain now serves unrelated gambling content and is rejected; Yupp's official wind-down blog returned 403. A functioning old URL is not necessarily an authentic surviving company source.

Public source availability, English-language reporting, funding publicity and founder willingness to disclose all bias discovery toward visible venture-backed firms. Microbusinesses and independent teams may disclose only product changes or anonymous anecdotes. Founder/maintainer discussions and independent communities are useful leads, but a personal side project, stopped product, acquisition, legal company closure and layoffs require separate evidence. This batch excludes anonymous layoffs and does not estimate how many unreported small firms were affected.

JSON structure, unique candidate/source IDs, HTTPS URL syntax, source references, category totals and unpublished flags were checked locally. Names have no exact normalized match against the current seed. Network access here was through the research web tool; these observations do not establish successful retrieval by the application's importer. Follow-up daily discovery can add candidates without changing these evidentiary boundaries.
